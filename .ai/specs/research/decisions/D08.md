# D08 — VikingDB remains the AI knowledge-retrieval target
- Date, owner: 2026-09-21, Kamil Mastalerz (decided in the discovery session, round 2)
- Context and the options weighed: ADR-0003 (accepted 2026-09-12) makes VikingDB the AI knowledge base with per-tenant collections; docs/production-readiness-plan.md §2 keeps knowledge content in PostgreSQL with no additional copies and does not mention VikingDB; the shipped prototype selects knowledge by local word matching with no embeddings.
- Decision and why: ADR-0003 stays active as the retrieval target. The readiness plan's knowledge clause is scoped to storage (content lives in PostgreSQL, no `.md` copies), not to retrieval; moving retrieval onto VikingDB is a Later slice.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: the word-matching prototype behavior is an interim state, not the architecture. Revisit only via a superseding row against ADR-0003.
- Status: active
