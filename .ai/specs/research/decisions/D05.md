# D05 — One permission level for admitted staff
- Date, owner: 2026-09-21 (decision recorded in docs/production-readiness-plan.md §4, written 2026-09-04); ownership confirmed by Kamil Mastalerz in the discovery session
- Context and the options weighed: role ladder (agent/admin/platform-admin, as the api's guards already model) vs. one level at launch.
- Decision and why: every admitted (allowlisted, verified) employee gets access to all mailboxes and settings, including changing the AI key. No finer roles in the first production version.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: the api's TenantRoleGuard/PlatformAdminGuard stay, but the deployed configuration grants one effective level. Revisit when the first real team needs separation.
- Status: active
